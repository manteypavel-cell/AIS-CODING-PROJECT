Our History

A Proud Heritage of Learning

Founded with a Vision

Akosombo International School (AIS) was established to provide world-class education within the serene and picturesque environment of Akosombo, in the Eastern Region of Ghana. The school was founded with a clear mission: to nurture young minds into confident, compassionate, and competent individuals ready to excel in a rapidly changing world.

From its beginnings, AIS has grown into a prestigious institution known for its rigorous academic programmes, strong moral grounding, and a vibrant community spirit. Over the years, the school has expanded its facilities, enriched its curriculum, and built a reputation as one of the leading international schools in the region.

Today, AIS proudly serves students from Nursery through to Senior High School (SHS), offering a seamless educational journey that lays a solid foundation in the early years and culminates in outstanding WASSCE results and university placements.
